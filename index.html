About Us

A new name in Hyderabad building on solid ground.

Eminent Infra Developers was founded in 2024 in Hyderabad with a simple premise: construction quality shouldn't be negotiable, and timelines shouldn't be a surprise. In our first two years, we've completed 2 projects totaling around 70,000 sq. ft., with a third project currently underway and new projects launching soon.

We're a lean, hands-on team — every project is personally overseen from design through handover, so nothing gets lost between contractors.

We work RERA-compliant on our residential projects, and our site engineering keeps clients updated on real progress at every stage of construction.
